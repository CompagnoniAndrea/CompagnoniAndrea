### About me

I'm an MSc student in Computer Science & Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, focusing on **Artificial Intelligence**. 

As a Graduate Researcher at **NECSTLab**, I'm working on a Graph Matching Network for drug discovery that aims to use cross-attention between protein and ligand graphs to improve compound–protein affinity prediction and binding interpretability.

I'm curious about the mechanisms behind learning and intelligence: how principles from nature, such as selection, reinforcement and adaptation, can be built into AI systems. Looking ahead, I’d love to dig deeper into Reinforcement Learning and evolutionary algorithms, to see how reward and selection can shape smarter autonomous systems. I’m also fascinated by multi-agent systems and the many real-world applications of Generative AI.

I volunteered at the Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics. <br>
Outside engineering, I love sports and nature, I play football and enjoy hiking.
